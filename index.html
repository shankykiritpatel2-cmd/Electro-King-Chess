📜 FOUR KINGDOM RULES

1. Turn Order: Red (South) ➡️ Blue (West) ➡️ Gold (North) ➡️ Green (East).

2. Board Geometry: 14x14 cross board with the 4 corner 3x3 areas removed.

3. Pawn Movement: Pawns march forward relative to kingdom orientation and capture diagonally forward. Pawns promote to Queens upon crossing the center into rank 8 from spawn.

4. Elimination: When a Kingdom's King is captured, that Kingdom is eliminated from the realm.

5. Victory: The last surviving Kingdom conquers the realm!
