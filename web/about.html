About ELECTRO KING

Welcome to ELECTRO KING — where strategy meets technology!

ELECTRO KING is a chess gaming project created with the goal of making chess enjoyable, accessible, and exciting through a modern digital experience.

We believe chess is more than just a board game. It develops strategic thinking, patience, concentration, and decision-making. Our aim is to bring these experiences into a modern chess platform with a distinctive cyber-electric identity.

Our Story

ELECTRO KING was created by Viaan Patel, with Shanky Patel as its publisher.

The project started with an interest in chess, technology, and creating something that players could genuinely enjoy. What began as a chess game project is being developed into a more complete, professional chess gaming experience.

What ELECTRO KING Offers

ELECTRO KING is designed to provide features such as:

  • Interactive Chess Gameplay: High-performance FIDE-compliant rules engine with smooth move animations and checkmate detection.
  • Calibrated AI Opponents: Four distinct bots ranging from beginner to Candidate Master level:
    • ⚡ Sparky (~600 Elo) — Friendly beginner sparring partner.
    • 🛡️ CyberKnight (~1200 Elo) — Solid tactical club player.
    • 👑 VoltQueen (~1700 Elo) — Advanced calculation and sharp openings.
    • 🌌 ElectroKing (~2200 Elo) — Ruthless Candidate Master engine.
  • 5 Dynamic Daily Puzzles: Five new, unique, and verified checkmate challenges generated every calendar day (resetting at midnight IST / Asia/Kolkata).
  • Multiple Chessboard Themes: High-voltage Electro, Obsidian, Gold Volt, and Matrix terminal aesthetics.
  • Player Profiles & Career Records: Points, streaks, achievements, win rates, and head-to-head records against all opponents.
  • Android Home-Screen Widgets: 4 native launcher widget configurations for quick match start and puzzle previews.
  • Modern Cross-Platform Design: Responsive layouts optimized for mobile devices, desktop PC browsers, and Android apps.
