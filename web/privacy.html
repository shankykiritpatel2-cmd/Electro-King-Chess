Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 21 November 2026 • Last Updated: 21 November 2026

Welcome to ELECTRO KING!

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ELECTRO KING handles information when you visit or use our chess game and related services.

By using ELECTRO KING, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

1. About ELECTRO KING

ELECTRO KING is a chess gaming platform created by Viaan Patel and published by Shanky Patel.

The platform is designed to provide users with an enjoyable chess-playing experience, including chess gameplay, AI challenges, daily tactical puzzles, and related gaming features.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ELECTRO KING may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Player names or display names: Chosen by you during match setup, guest mode, or account settings.
  • Profile pictures or avatars: Selected or cropped from your device and stored locally using your browser's IndexedDB/localStorage.
  • Chess game statistics and match history: Matches played, victories, defeats, draws, win rates, streaks, and computer opponent records.
  • Puzzle progress & streaks: Daily puzzles completed, accuracy, points earned, and streak data.
  • Game preferences and settings: Board themes, sound effects, haptic vibrations, coordinates, evaluation bar, and orientation choices.
  • Authentication data: When using Google or Email Sign-In, basic account identifiers (email, account creation date, unique Player ID) are processed to secure your account.
  • Technical information: Diagnostic data required to operate, troubleshoot, and maintain platform stability (such as anonymous crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics on Android).
  • Support enquiries: Information voluntarily provided when contacting support.

The information actually collected depends on the specific features enabled in the version you use.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate interactive chess gameplay and AI opponents.
  • Verify and award points for daily tactical puzzles and achievements.
  • Save and restore game preferences, board themes, and widget styling.
  • Display player profiles, career records, and match history.
  • Enable cross-device cloud synchronization for authenticated players.
  • Improve game performance, responsiveness, and user experience.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems and application crashes.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Maintain platform security and protect against fraudulent activity.

4. Local Storage

Certain settings and game information are stored locally on your device or browser using localStorage and IndexedDB.

Clearing browser data, uninstalling the application, or resetting your device will remove locally stored information unless synchronized with a cloud account.

When online accounts or cloud-saving features are used, player progress is synchronized securely with supported backend cloud services.

5. Third-Party Services

ELECTRO KING may use third-party services to support functionality such as hosting (GitHub Pages), database & authentication (Firebase / Google Play Services), and crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics).

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Advertising

ELECTRO KING is currently free and does not display intrusive advertisements. If Google AdMob or another advertising provider is integrated in future updates, that provider may collect device and app interaction data subject to its policies and applicable consent requirements. We will update this policy accordingly if advertising is introduced.

7. Children's Privacy

ELECTRO KING aims to provide a safe, family-friendly, and enjoyable chess experience. We do not intentionally request unnecessary personal information from children under 13.

Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with a child may contact us at any time. If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to promptly address and remove the issue.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by ELECTRO KING. However, no online service, internet transmission, or electronic storage system can guarantee complete security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

Users may reset their local statistics at any time from the Profile settings. If you have an authenticated account and wish to delete your account and associated cloud data, you can do so directly from Account Settings or by contacting us at the email address below.

10. Your Choices

Depending on the features available, you may be able to:

  • Change your display name at any time.
  • Change or remove your profile picture.
  • Update game sound, haptic, and visual preferences.
  • Clear locally stored game information via the Reset Statistics button.
  • Switch between Guest Mode and authenticated cloud accounts.
  • Contact us with privacy-related requests or questions.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ELECTRO KING introduces new features, services, or updates to its data practices. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.
