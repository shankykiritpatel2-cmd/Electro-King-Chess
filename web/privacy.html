ELECTRO KING ⚡
Official Privacy Policy

Created by Viaan Patel • Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Electro King is built with a privacy-first philosophy. We do not collect personal accounts, do not track your personal data, and do not sell any information. Your chess games, statistics, and avatars remain strictly on your own device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Electro King is designed so that players can enjoy chess without sacrificing privacy:

  • No Personal Identification: We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or real names.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or request your GPS or device location.
  • No Image/Media Uploads: When you customize your profile picture, the image is cropped and saved locally in your browser's IndexedDB / local storage. It is never uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Financial Data: Electro King is completely free and contains no paid subscriptions or credit card processing.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored purely locally on your device to enable game features:

  • Game Progress & Stats: Win/Loss/Draw record, Elo difficulty achievements, tactical puzzle solved counts, and match move history.
  • Preferences: Board sound effects, move animations, theme styling, and widget settings.
  • Custom Avatar: Your selected profile picture stored client-side.

You can completely erase this data at any time by clearing your browser cache / app storage.

3. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

The Android version of Electro King may utilize Google Firebase Crashlytics solely for stability and error monitoring:

  • Crashlytics: In the event of an unexpected crash, Crashlytics collects anonymous system diagnostic information (device model, OS version, and stack traces) to help developers patch bugs. No personally identifiable information is collected.

4. Device Permissions

Electro King requests only the minimal permissions required for standard functionality:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for loading web assets, receiving service worker offline updates, and delivering crash diagnostics.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when pieces move or are captured.

Electro King does not request access to Camera, Contacts, Microphone, Storage, or SMS.

5. Children's Privacy

Electro King complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy standards. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we do not collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
